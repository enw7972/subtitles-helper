2. Live speech capture (experimental)

Important: Safari speech recognition listens through the microphone; it does not directly read the video's internal audio track. For best chance of recognition, play the video through the device speaker, turn off headphones, allow microphone access, and keep the device close. Browser support and offline operation are not guaranteed. Timestamps are approximate.

Auto-pause is intended for proofreading. Resume playback manually after editing.

5. Load in Web Video Cast

  1. Export SRT and save it to Files.
  2. Open your video in Web Video Cast and cast to Roku.
  3. Open its subtitle control, choose Local File / previously downloaded subtitles, and select the SRT file.

If your app version does not show Local File, use its available subtitle-import option.

Local file selection and export happen in your browser. Speech recognition may use Apple or browser services; this page cannot guarantee fully offline processing.
